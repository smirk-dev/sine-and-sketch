Where this comes from

    
One question, and the person who answers it best

    
Sine & Sketch started from a single question: how can a formula hold a whole picture? The best answer I have seen is the work of Hamid Naderi Yeganeh, and this studio takes heavy inspiration from it. His swarms of shapes indexed by k, his networks of functions that decide the colour of every pixel, and his habit of treating each image as a function are the three ideas under everything here. It is a tribute, not a copy. His own formulas are mostly unpublished, so the examples are my attempts at the same idea. If this makes you curious, go and look at his work first.


    

      

        
        

          
The inspiration

          
Hamid Naderi Yeganeh

          
Iranian mathematician and mathematical artist, born in Qom in 1990. He studied at the University of Qom and Sharif University of Technology and has a PhD from University College London. He draws birds, boats, faces, fractals and tessellations using nothing but mathematical formulas. His artwork 9,000 Ellipses was the cover of The American Mathematical Monthly in November 2017.
