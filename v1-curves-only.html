How a sketch becomes math

The method behind the mathematical drawings: build a picture out of sine and cosine, then tune coefficients until it looks right. Here the fitting is done for you.

01

Trace

Your stroke is resampled into 256 evenly spaced points along its length, measured in plane units, so the equation lives on the same axes you see.

02

Decompose

Closed shapes get a Fourier series on t ∈ [0, 2π]. Open strokes get a cosine series on t ∈ [0, π]. The harmonics slider keeps the strongest terms.

03

Write & replay

The result is a parametric pair (x(t), y(t)). Edit any number, or add families with k, and the plane redraws instantly.
